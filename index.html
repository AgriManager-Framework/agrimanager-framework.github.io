Beyond Distribution Shift: Schema Shift

Traditional RL generalization focuses largely on changing environments under a fixed task interface. But what happens when the interface itself changes?

Distribution shift

Same interface, different context

  • WeatherNormal→Drought
  • CropSeen→Unseen
  • PriceTypical→High corn price
Schema shift

The task interface itself changes

  • ObservationFull→Partial
  • ActionN + Water→N only
  • RewardYield→Profit

Σ=(ΣOobservation,ΣAaction,ΣRreward)

We formalize schema shift as a change in the policy-facing schema Σ between training and deployment.

Why Study Schema Shift in Agriculture?

Farms vary in their task interface, and every decision plays out over a season or years.

Varying task interfacesFarms differ in what they observe, can do, and aim for.
Long-term consequencesDecisions pay off at harvest, or years later.
Simulation-driven evaluationDSSAT, WOFOST and Cycles allow reproducible training and testing.

Where Generalization Works — and Where It Breaks

Tier 1

Within a fixed interface, RL generalizes

With explicit reasoning, a Qwen3-4B policy trained by RL improves over the untrained model on every held-out weather regime, crop and price regime, and outperforms a neural-network PPO baseline trained with the same budget. Without reasoning, training often stalls near doing nothing.

Tier 2

Different schema shifts, different bottlenecks

Observations

Renaming, adding or dropping secondary fields barely matters; removing the resource state (soil water and nutrients) breaks the policy.

Actions

The policy handles a new menu if it has seen those actions together before.1

Trained onNPKWNPK →NW Improves over the untrained model
Trained onNPKW →NW Falls below the untrained model
N, P, K: nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium fertilizer · W: irrigation water

1 Consistent with joint action coverage; menu wrappers also differ (paper §4.2).

Objectives

Trained on yield alone, the policy transfers to profit, water and nutrient goals it never saw. Trained on several goals at once, only the reasoning policy learns reliably.

Tier 3

Composition remains open

Switching simulators

A policy trained on one simulator improves on that simulator, but on another simulator it usually ends up worse than before training.

Recombining components

One policy is trained on many simulator, action menu and goal combinations, then tested on a combination it never saw. On DSSAT it gets about 86% of the way to a specialist trained on that exact combination. On WOFOST and Cycles it stays below the untrained model.
