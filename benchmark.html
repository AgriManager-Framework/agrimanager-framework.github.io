A Three-Tier Generalization Benchmark

AgriManager systematically evaluates how reinforcement learning policies generalize across changing environments and task interfaces. Eight settings, organized into three tiers, give a structured testbed for understanding where policy generalization succeeds and where it breaks.

Evaluation protocol

  

    
6,278test-suite rows: every policy is scored on the same frozen, held-out evaluation rows (rows, not independent physical scenarios)

    
3training seeds per method, each scored at its terminal checkpoint with no selection on validation

    
2reference policies: no-action (0) and a scripted agronomic reference (1) that anchor normalized return

  

  

    
Frozen held-out test suite
All headline scores come from a prespecified suite of 6,278 evaluation rows, built after submission and disjoint from each experiment's training and validation data. Rows are not independent physical scenarios.

    
Three seeds, terminal checkpoints
Each LLM-RL arm and neural baseline trains three seeds. Every run is scored at its terminal checkpoint with no selection on validation; we report mean ± SD over seeds.

    
Normalized return
R̃(π) = (R(π) − Rno-action) / |Rref − Rno-action|
No-action sits at 0 and the scripted agronomic reference at 1. The gap varies across targets, so policies are compared by R̃ only within a target.

    
Baselines
The untrained model (the same backbone before RL), neural-network PPO with the interaction budget of LLM-RL on the Tier 1 settings, the scripted agronomic reference, and no-action.

    
Confirmatory and exploratory results
Three-seed test-suite results are confirmatory. Results only on validation or from a single seed (training curves, backbone comparisons) and the per-crop breakdown are exploratory and labeled as such.
