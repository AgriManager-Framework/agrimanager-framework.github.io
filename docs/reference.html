Citation

How to cite AgriManager, and what it builds on.

If you use AgriManager, please cite the paper (links to the proceedings and arXiv versions will be added here). A machine-readable entry is in CITATION.cff.

bibtex
@inproceedings{agrimanager2026,
  title     = {AgriManager: A Framework and Benchmark for LLM-RL
               Generalization in Agricultural Management},
  author    = {Gui, Chi and Zheng, Junwen and Nigam, Ritwik and Yang, Qianlan and
               Lang, Meagan and Dongre, Vardhan and Barr, Jeremiah and
               Wang, Yu-Xiong and Adve, Vikram S.},
  booktitle = {Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS),
               Evaluations and Datasets Track},
  year      = {2026}
}

Acknowledgements#

AgriManager builds on VeRL, WOFOST-Gym, CycleGym, and gym-DSSAT, and on the WOFOST, Cycles and DSSAT crop models. Thanks to these projects and their maintainers. When you use a simulator through AgriManager, please also cite the simulator and its wrapper.
