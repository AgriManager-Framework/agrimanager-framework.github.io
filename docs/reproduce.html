Reproducing the paper

From checkpoint revisions to the CSV rows behind every table and figure.

Every number in the paper traces to a row of a CSV table in results/tables/, and every table traces to scenario-level records produced by scoring a named checkpoint revision on the frozen held-out test suite.

StepScriptWhat it does
Scoreentrypoints/eval/e5_evaluate_checkpoint.sh; run_e4_anchors.sh; run_eval_*nn*_e4.shone result bundle per checkpoint revision and setting (Evaluation)
Extractanalysis/test_suite/extract_e4.py --data-root <result roots> --out <dir>reads every bundle, takes the metric each split declares as primary, writes one row per scenario, and checks each split's mean against the evaluator's own summary
Tabulateanalysis/test_suite/e4_tables.py --episodes <dir>/e4_episodes.parquetselects each seed's terminal checkpoint, computes cell statistics, paired and between-method comparisons, reference gaps and normalized scores
Drawanalysis/test_suite/make_figures.pyredraws the three tier figures from the CSVs into results/figures/; the paper's final-layout figure scripts are kept with the paper sources

results/scoring_manifest.tsv lists every checkpoint revision scored for the paper with its evaluator arguments; feed it to entrypoints/tools/e4_score_worker.sh --queue to re-score. NN results are read from experiments/*/results/nn_eval_e4/, or from the directory given with --nn-root.

Per-run records

The final tables in results/tables/ combine the September 2026 scoring with the October 2026 re-scoring of the Tier 3 Cycles cells on the rebuilt suites. Part of the September per-run bundles and the scenario-level episode file were lost with a storage allocation; the aggregate tables survived. The bundles are being regenerated by re-scoring the revisions in results/scoring_manifest.tsv. Until that finishes, some cells reproduce only from the aggregate CSVs.

Statistics#

  • Cells. Mean and SD over the three training seeds of an arm; each seed's value is its mean over the scenarios of the split.
  • Think versus no-think. Paired by seed (the arms share the training seed) and by scenario.
  • Between methods (LLM-RL versus NN, unified versus specialist). Seeds are treated as independent; the difference is reported in units of the propagated SD, sqrt(sd_a² + sd_b²).
  • Confirmatory and exploratory. Three-seed test-suite results are confirmatory. Results only on validation or from one seed are exploratory and labelled as such, for example training curves and the backbone comparisons.
